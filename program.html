Program

Three days. One historic hall.

February 5–7, 2027 at Niwot Hall. Full lineup and additional programming to be announced.

Schedule

Friday, February 5

Opening ceremony

The festival opens at Niwot Hall with a ceremony and conversation with Southern Arapaho and Cheyenne tribal leaders.

Saturday–Sunday, February 6–7

Film screenings

Four confirmed films screen at Niwot Hall over the weekend. Full lineup and showtimes to be announced.

Venue

All 2027 programming takes place at Niwot Hall — the oldest active Grange hall in Colorado, built in 1873. Stay tuned for more details, including the full film lineup.
